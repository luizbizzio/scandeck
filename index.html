Games inside codes.

ScanDeck is a small game console in your browser.

Most QR codes open a website. ScanDeck is different. The code contains the game itself. Scan it here, and play.

Beta version. For safety, only play game codes from sources you trust. Very large codes may be hard to scan with a phone.

How it works

  1. 01Find a ScanDeck game code.
  2. 02Scan it or upload its image.
  3. 03Play in your browser.

More space for bigger games.
